Retail Sales & Profitability Intelligence

SQL, Python, Excel and Tableau. 18 stores, 133,000 transactions, 2024–2025.

The question
Revenue was growing, but management didn't know which stores and products actually made money.
What I found
Operating profit was just 0.7% of sales. All 5 Express stores lose money, discounts equal 47% of gross profit without lifting units sold, and 13 products sell below cost.
What I recommended
Fix or close the Express stores, cap discounts at 25%, and reprice the below-cost products. The Express fix alone would more than double operating profit.
